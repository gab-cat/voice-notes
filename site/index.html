It reads voice notes. It doesn't take orders from them.

				
A voice note is someone else's words. Voice Notes treats them as content to transcribe, never as instructions.


				

					$agy -p "…" --model gemini-3.8-flash-medium --sandbox --output-format json
				


				

					

						
No auto-approve

						
It never passes --dangerously-skip-permissions. In headless runs, anything that needs approval, like running a command or editing a file, is refused.

					

					

						
Sandboxed

						
Each run gets --sandbox on top, and only sees the folder for that one note.

					

					

						
Your notes stay on your Mac

						
Transcripts, ticks and chats live in Application Support. The audio goes to Google through Antigravity to be transcribed, and nowhere else.

Questions, answered.

					
Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

				

				

					

						Does my audio leave my Mac?
						
Yes, once: it goes to Google through the Antigravity CLI so Gemini can transcribe it. The transcript, summary, ticks and chats are stored on your Mac, in Application Support.

					

					

						Does it work with the WhatsApp app, or only WhatsApp Web?
						
Either. It watches your Downloads folder for files named WhatsApp Audio, so any way of saving the voice note there works.

					

					

						Which languages does it understand?
						
Whatever Gemini understands, including notes that switch between languages mid-sentence. When a note isn't in English you also get an English translation.

					

					

						Will it reply for me?
						
No. The chat drafts replies ready to paste into WhatsApp. Sending is always up to you.

					

					

						What about voice notes I already have?
						
The first time it runs, it lists what's already in Downloads without sending anything. Each one has a Transcribe button for when you want it.

					

					

						Why does macOS warn me when I open it?
						
The app is signed ad hoc rather than notarized by Apple, which is common for small open-source tools. You allow it once. After an update, macOS may ask about Downloads again.

					

					

						Does it cost anything?
						
The app is free and open source. Transcription runs on your own Antigravity account, under whatever limits Google sets for it.
